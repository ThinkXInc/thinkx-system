Introducing Our Revised Organizational Structure

Established in January 2015 as an advanced IT research and development entity, 6th Sense, Ltd. (President: Kazuki Otsuka) has embarked on an organizational metamorphosis.
As of December 2019, we have transitioned into ThinkX,Inc, ushering in a new era of our organization with a focus on software services.
Our renewed focus will be on the consolidation and development of AI-related technologies we have fostered till date.
These include, but are not limited to, natural language processing for speech, recommendation technology, large-scale distributed processing infrastructure, cryptography technology, and over 1.1 million events of structured wiki data.

We eagerly anticipate this exciting chapter and remain steadfast in our commitment to delivering exceptional service.
