2020 Feb 7

We are pleased to announce our participation in the "Regional Development EXPO" to be held at Makuhari Messe in February 2021.

CITYWALK Project will exhibit at the "Regional Development Expo"
to be held at Makuhari Messe next February 2020 to strengthen the destination marketing support system

as one solution to the declining population and to contribute to regional development nationwide.

Based on discussions with local governments and tourism bureaus in Nagasaki, Himeji, Okinawa City,
and other cities, as well as the results of our research on DMOs,
we have prepared a "Local Government Special Plan" for local governments with complex budgeting needs.



The average per capita consumption of Japanese people is said to be 1 million yen,
If the population decreases by 100 people, the economy shrinks by 100 million yen.

CITYWALK is a tool that supports digital marketing, which lags far behind other developed countries,
and connects the flow of visitors from outside the region, their circulation within the region,

and their introductions and return visits through word of mouth.
