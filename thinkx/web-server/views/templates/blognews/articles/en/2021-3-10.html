Second Nagasaki City VLOG, Featuring Chinese KOL 摇君柠, Now Live on Weibo, Japan's Leading SNS Platform

Weibo, China's largest social networking platform, is thrilled to announce the release of a new video: an inbound tour of Nagasaki City. This video, co-produced by our team and Chinese KOL Jianle Wang (摇君柠), who is based in Japan and boasts over 510,000 followers, has achieved considerable success.

Within the first five days of its release, the video accumulated 1.3 million views (2.33 million PV)..
It was also featured on the account "林萍在日本," which has an impressive 5.74 million followers.

Under the theme of "Finding China in Japan," our objective was to showcase Nagasaki as a city with special significance for the Chinese population—a destination that Chinese travelers can relate to. We aimed to tell the city's unique story,
highlighting not just its beauty and intrigue, but also how it aligns with its characteristic atmosphere, which is crucial for attracting visitors.

It's unusual for an official promotional video to be produced in the full-length, high-resolution Ultra HD 4K vlog format. However, for this project, we embraced advanced editing techniques and the latest video equipment to create a trailblazing vlog video.

On Weibo, the video elicited comments such as,"I never knew that Nagasaki has such a deep connection with China. It's fascinating—I hope to visit in person someday," and
"The kimonos and scenery are stunning. I'll definitely return once the pandemic subsides."

Total views, as of March 16, 2021, stand at 1.3 million (2.66 million PV).
