CITYWALK and Its Founder, Representative Otsuka, Featured on KTN Evening News

Our groundbreaking voice guidance system, CITYWALK, currently active in Nagasaki City, was recently featured on KTN's evening news. The broadcast showcased responses from users, along with highlights from a briefing session held for local groups at Dejima.
Comments from users and the briefing session held at Dejima for groups in the city were introduced.

We are deeply grateful for this opportunity, which brought CITYWALK and President Otsuka's innovative vision into the spotlight.
