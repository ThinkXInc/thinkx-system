Introducing Our New Promotional VLOG: Explore Nagasaki City with Influencers in Stunning 4K Resolution

We are thrilled to present a 4K VLOG video (available in both Japanese and English) that promotes Nagasaki City in an engaging, immersive way. Featuring "hidden gems" known primarily to locals and gathered through four separate on-site surveys, the video received more than 10,000 views within three days of being published on YouTube.
This innovative approach proposes a new kind of promotional strategy, one that is intimately connected to the CITYWALK experience.

The high-quality 4K VLOG video has prompted enthusiastic responses, with comments such as "I want to visit Nagasaki - I aim to see all the prefectures," and "I felt like I was watching a travel show due to the exceptional storytelling, visuals, and superior production quality."

One of the unique features of this video is its integration with the CITYWALK platform.
This allows viewers who think, "I want to experience that, too," to directly navigate to the destination and enjoy the experience through CITYWALK's guidance, marking a world-first initiative of this kind.

Shortly after the video's release, local staff members received inquiries from individuals wishing to "capture a photo at the same spot as Tomii," the influencer featured in the video, or "try on the same outfit."
We believe that this demonstrates the real-world impact of this innovative promotional strategy on local residents.

The video, titled "NAGASAKI: Where Foreign and Japanese Cultures Merge (4K Vlog/English subtitle)," can be viewed on Tomii's channel.

「NAGASAKI Where foreign and Japanese cultures merge / 長崎 - 海外と日本の文化の合流する場所 【4K Vlog/English subtitle】」とみいちゃんねる 



Our dedication to highlighting the unique charm of this region to a global audience remains steadfast.
