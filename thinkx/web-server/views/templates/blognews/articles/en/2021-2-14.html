Groundbreaking Voice Guidance System, CITYWALK, Commences Pilot Program in Nagasaki City

Our pioneering voice guidance system, CITYWALK, has commenced its pilot program at popular tourist destinations such as Glover Garden,
Dejima, and i+Land Nagasaki, which collectively draw an annual crowd of 400,000.

Visitors can simply scan the QR code at the entrance to download the system onto their iPhones (with Android support planned within the year). Upon checking in,
they can enjoy a guided experience as they navigate the premises, with the system automatically offering direction and insights.

This trial run is part of a Japan Tourism Agency project in Nagasaki City and will continue for one month, ending on March 15.


For additional information or to learn more about our product, please contact us at inquiry@thinkxinc.com.
